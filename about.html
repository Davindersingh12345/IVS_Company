MEET OUR FOUNDER

John Anderson

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

John started his career with a simple belief: transportation should be built around reliability, communication and trust. After years of experience in transportation and logistics, he founded IVS to create a company where customers know their freight is handled with care from pickup to delivery.

His leadership continues to focus on building a dependable team, maintaining a modern fleet and creating long-term relationships with customers and transportation partners.

“Every shipment represents a promise. Our job is to keep that promise.”
